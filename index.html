Our Master-Class Electrical Repair Services

A deep dive into our core competencies. We handle equipment that others declare "beyond repair".

1. Premium Electric Motor Rewinding & Rebuilding

Electric motors are the beating heart of any manufacturing plant. From single-phase water pump motors to massive three-phase industrial slip-ring motors and high-precision servo motors, we handle it all. Our motor rewinding process is meticulous.

We completely strip down the motor and bake the stator in a controlled temperature oven to safely remove old resin without damaging the core laminations. We strictly use Class H and Class F insulation materials and 100% pure copper wire. After rewinding, the motor is vacuum pressure impregnated (VPI) with high-grade epoxy varnish to ensure it can withstand severe industrial environments, moisture, and high temperatures.

Common Motor Issues We Fix: Winding burnout due to phase loss, rotor bar cracking, worn-out housing, bearing journal damage, and excessive vibration.

2. Power & Distribution Transformer Maintenance

Your transformer is the gateway of power to your entire facility. A transformer failure doesn't just stop one machine; it blacks out your entire plant. Our pan-India transformer repair services cover everything from routine oil filtration to complete core rebuilding.

Over time, transformer oil absorbs moisture and generates sludge, drastically reducing its dielectric strength. This leads to internal arcing and catastrophic failure. Our mobile oil filtration units can come to your site anywhere in India to dehydrate, degas, and filter your transformer oil while the transformer is energized. If your transformer has already suffered a short circuit, our heavy lifting workshop can rebuild the HV and LV coils, replace the gaskets to stop oil leakages, and conduct mandatory Megger and Ratio tests.

Key Transformer Services: Oil breakdown voltage (BDV) testing, silica gel breather replacement, tap changer repair, and bushing replacement.

3. CNC Panel, VFD, & PLC Troubleshooting

Modern factories rely heavily on automation. But when a printed circuit board (PCB) inside your Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) blows, or your PLC logic gets corrupted, mechanical mechanics are useless. You need electronics and automation experts.

Our rapid-response automation team specializes in diagnosing control panel issues. We deal with complex wiring faults, blown contactors, faulty relays, and erratic servo drive behaviors. We understand the language of automated machinery. Whether your CNC machine is throwing an obscure error code, or your packaging machine's sensors have stopped communicating with the main panel, we have the diagnostic tools to pinpoint the faulty component on the micro-level, saving you from buying a costly new drive.

Supported Brands: Siemens, ABB, Allen-Bradley, Mitsubishi, Delta, and Fanuc.

Our Scientific Repair Protocol

We don't guess. We test, diagnose, and rebuild following strict engineering protocols.

When you hand over your expensive machinery to us, you need to know exactly what happens inside our workshop. Here is our transparent, 5-step repair process:


  • Step 1: Initial Electrical & Mechanical Inspection

    We don't dismantle anything until we test it. We check the insulation resistance using a Megger, measure phase-to-phase resistance, and check for mechanical shaft run-out. We document the incoming condition meticulously.

  • Step 2: Careful Dismantling & Root Cause Analysis

    The machine is disassembled using specialized pullers to avoid damaging bearing housings. We don't just look at *what* is broken; we determine *why* it broke. Did the motor burn due to a power surge, or did a failing bearing cause the rotor to rub against the stator? Finding the root cause prevents future failures.

  • Step 3: Precision Repair & Rewinding

    If rewinding is required, old coils are carefully recorded for turns and wire gauge. New coils are wound on automated CNC winding machines for perfect tension. Mechanical parts like worn bearing housings are built up using metal spray and machined back to exact OEM tolerances on our lathes.

  • Step 4: Assembly & Dynamic Balancing

    Rotors and armatures are dynamically balanced on computerized balancing machines to eliminate vibration. We install high-grade SKF or FAG bearings, heating them uniformly using induction heaters before sliding them onto the shaft. No hammers are ever used.

  • Step 5: Final Load Testing & Quality Assurance

    The assembled machine is run on our test bed. We monitor voltage, current, temperature rise, and vibration levels over several hours. Only when the machine passes these stringent parameters do we apply our industrial-grade paint and dispatch it back to your factory.

The Ultimate Industrial Machine Troubleshooting Guide

Are you facing an issue right now? Read our expert insights on the most common electrical machine problems.

1. Why is my Electric Motor Overheating?

Heating is the number one killer of electric machines. For every 10°C rise above the motor's rated operating temperature, the insulation life is cut in half. Overheating is typically caused by three things: Overloading (the machine is being asked to do more mechanical work than it was designed for), Poor Ventilation (dust and debris have blocked the cooling fins or the cooling fan is broken), or Voltage Imbalance. Even a 3% voltage imbalance between your three phases can cause a 25% increase in heat generation. If you touch your motor and it is too hot to hold your hand on for more than a few seconds, you need to shut it down and call our team immediately.

2. What causes excessive Vibration and Noise?

Electrical machines should run with a smooth hum. If you hear grinding, squealing, or feel heavy vibrations, mechanical failure is imminent. This is almost always related to bearings. Bearings fail due to lack of lubrication, over-lubrication (which blows out the seals), or misalignment between the motor shaft and the load it is driving. Another common cause of vibration is a "soft foot"—where the motor is not sitting perfectly flat on its mounting base, causing the frame to twist when the bolts are tightened. Our laser alignment tools can fix this instantly on-site.

3. The Motor trips the breaker as soon as it starts

If your machine immediately trips the MCB or MCCB upon startup, you have a direct short circuit. This could be a "phase-to-phase" short (the insulation between two windings has melted away), or a "phase-to-ground" short (a winding has touched the metal casing of the motor). Do not attempt to reset the breaker and try again; you will only cause more explosive damage to the internals. You need an immediate Megger test (insulation resistance test) to confirm the integrity of the windings. Call our 24/7 emergency line, and we will dispatch a technician.

4. Control Panel VFD keeps throwing an "Over-Voltage" or "Under-Voltage" fault

Variable Frequency Drives are highly sensitive to power quality. If your VFD trips on over-voltage during deceleration, your machine's load is acting like a generator (regenerative energy), and you may need a dynamic braking resistor installed. If it trips on under-voltage, you might have severe voltage dips in your factory's main power supply when other heavy machinery starts up. Our panel engineers can install line reactors and harmonic filters to clean up your power supply and keep your automated lines running smoothly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Detailed answers to the most common queries regarding industrial electrical repair in India.

How quickly can your team reach our factory in case of an emergency breakdown?

If your facility is located within major industrial hubs like Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad, or Chennai, our rapid response team can usually be on-site within 2 to 4 hours. For other locations across India, we arrange immediate dispatch of our engineering teams and can often reach within 12 to 24 hours depending on the logistics. We recommend utilizing our WhatsApp emergency line for the fastest response.

Do you use aluminum or copper wire for motor rewinding?

We strictly use 100% pure electrolytic grade copper wire for all our motor rewinding services. Many local repair shops use aluminum wire or low-grade copper alloys to cut costs, which drastically reduces the motor's efficiency and lifespan. We provide a complete material guarantee, ensuring your motor retains its original OEM specifications and energy efficiency ratings.

What is the difference between Class F and Class H insulation?

Insulation classes determine the maximum temperature an electrical winding can safely withstand before failing. Class F insulation is rated for up to 155°C, which is standard for most industrial motors. Class H is rated for up to 180°C. For heavy-duty applications like steel mills, kilns, or areas with high ambient temperatures, we highly recommend and utilize Class H insulation to provide a robust safety margin against overheating and burnout.

Can you repair obsolete or discontinued CNC control panels and VFDs?

Yes. Finding replacement parts for machinery that is 15-20 years old can be impossible. Our automation and electronics repair lab specializes in component-level repair. Instead of replacing the entire motherboard of an obsolete drive, we trace the circuitry and replace the specific blown transistors, thyristors, or logic chips, saving you from a highly expensive full-system retrofit.

What does an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) for electrical machines include?

Our standard AMC includes regular scheduled visits by our certified technicians. It covers thorough visual inspections, cleaning of cooling fans and fins, checking electrical connections for tightness (loose connections cause arcing and fires), measuring and recording voltage/current parameters, megger testing for insulation health, thermal imaging of panels to detect hotspots, and greasing bearings according to exact ultrasonic measurements.

How do I know if my transformer needs oil filtration?

Transformer oil should ideally be tested once a year. If the Breakdown Voltage (BDV) drops below 30kV, or if the moisture content (PPM) goes high, filtration is immediately required. Visually, if the oil looks dark, cloudy, or if the silica gel in the breather has turned from blue to pink (or orange to white, depending on the type), it means moisture has entered the system and you need our filtration services to prevent an internal short circuit.

We conduct vibration analysis, laser alignment, bearing replacements, panel troubleshooting, and transformer oil filtration right on your factory floor. Only complex jobs like full rewinding or core rebuilding require transporting the unit to our specialized workshops.

How does vibration analysis save money?

Vibration analysis is like an ECG for your machines. By placing sensors on the motor housing, our software can analyze the frequency of vibrations. It can tell us exactly if a bearing is starting to pit, if the shaft is bent, or if the rotor is out of balance. Finding this out *before* the bearing shatters means we can replace a ₹2,000 bearing during a planned Sunday shutdown, rather than dealing with a destroyed rotor, damaged stator, and days of lost production worth lakhs on a busy Tuesday.
